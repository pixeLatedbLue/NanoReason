No model is involved on this tab. That is the whole point: the process reward is a deterministic verifier, so you can paste any reasoning at all — written by a model, or by you — and it will be checked line by line on the CPU. Nothing is generated, nothing is guessed.

The sample above contains one deliberately wrong step. The verifier finds it without knowing the answer.

Every evaluation written to results/ is listed here exactly as it was recorded. Nothing on this tab is computed in the browser beyond formatting.

Evaluation runs

Compare two runs

A raw delta is not evidence. Two runs of 200 items can differ by four points from sampling noise alone, which is why each accuracy is shown with its Wilson interval and, where the same items were scored twice, a paired McNemar test — overlapping intervals or a large p-value mean the difference has not been demonstrated.
